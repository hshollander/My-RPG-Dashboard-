Your nine attributes

How attributes improve

Every week, the app reviews your recorded daily quests. Attributes improve when you consistently complete the activities linked to them. Poor consistency can reduce an attribute. You don't choose your own improvements.

Weekly review: +1 at 70% or above, -1 at 25% or below. Scores are capped between 0 and 100.
